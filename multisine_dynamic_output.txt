PREDICTIONS SCORECARD
================================================================================================

P1 (det ratio = 8/9 at M = 1, filter/frequency/theta-free; PROVEN, code check).  HELD.
   quadrature: |det ratio - 8/9| <= 1.1e-05 over the sweep; simulation: within 4.5e-03 of 8/9,
   i.e. the documented -0.44 Delta/C finite-threshold bias.  The T-congruence cancels in det, as proven.

P2 (lam_min ratio is metric-bound; full whitening restores 2/3, diagonal does not; PROVEN, code check).  HELD.
   raw ratio in [1.3300, 1.3333] -- not 2/3, above 1, pinned near 4/3 by the ill-conditioned
   M_per at M = 1; diag-normalised in [1.2733, 1.3331] -- moves with omega, never 2/3; full
   whitening S = M_per^(-1/2): lam_min = 2/3 to 1.7e-05 at every omega.  E is metric-bound; D is not.

P3 (event rate saturation).  HELD for m; lambda depends on the Delta rule.
   m = <|xdot|> is monotone (True) with NO interior maximum, saturating at 1.4256 vs the
   coherent two-lag plateau (2/pi)(th1/T1 + th2/T2) = 1.4324 (0.4% at omega = 20); the single-lag
   pieces are 1.2732 and 0.1592.  Both lags contribute ~1/(i w) phase at high omega, so
   they add coherently.  lambda = m/Delta itself keeps rising (Delta = C/100 shrinks like 1/omega):
   the flattening statement is about the rate at any FIXED threshold, and holds there.

P4 (does R11 + R22 = 2<E>_w/<E>_1 survive without the derivative relation?; OPEN).  IT DOES NOT.
   Across the Figure-2 grid: max|R11+R22 - 2<E_x>_w/<E_x>_1| = 0.76 (output envelope), 0.77
   (input envelope); the direction sum sits BELOW 2 almost everywhere while the envelope reading sits
   ABOVE 2, and their correlation over the grid is weak.  Already at M = 1 the sum is not 2 (e.g.
   1.5077 at omega = 1): the identity needed the regressors to be a quadrature pair (phase
   offset 90 deg), which [G1 u, G2 u] are not.  The equal-N det gain > 1 nevertheless SURVIVES (Figure
   2d: up to ~1.55 near omega_2/omega_1 ~ 1.4), so the envelope-sum identity was sufficient, not
   necessary, for the M > 1 gain.

BOTTOM LINE (the supervisor's question), at ONE FIXED threshold Delta = median(C_peak)/100 =
   0.01400 for every input -- the earlier variable-Delta headline conflated the Delta^-2
   scaling of det I with input design and is retracted; panel (b) keeps it only as a resolution
   study.  At comparable event rate (middle 40% of lambda), det I/T^2 spans p90/p10 = 4x.
   Still far beyond factor 2: the trade-off is real after the confound is removed.  Efficient inputs
   concentrate power within roughly a factor 3 of the filter corners 1/T1 = 2, 1/T2 = 0.5; high-
   frequency tones buy events but almost no information; a slightly-split pair beats a single tone
   at equal N (Figure 2d).
